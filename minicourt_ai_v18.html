Referee voice uses your device’s speech voice with a slower, formal umpire-style delivery. Automatic court scanning uses the visible white tape. The umpire engine follows the 2026 ITF rules where observable, adapted for your net-free practice court. All LET calls and let replays are disabled as requested. It tracks the whole two-colour ball using colour, shape, frame-to-frame movement and predicted position. Spinning should not require one coloured panel to face the camera. A line touch counts as IN. Each confirmed in-court bounce increments the current rally count; a second in-court bounce ends the point as DOUBLE BOUNCE. A confirmed bounce fully outside is OUT. If tracking is lost, it waits 15 seconds before ending the point. At point end it announces the rally length and resets the rally counter for the next point.

Rule basis: 2026 ITF Rules of Tennis. This is an adapted practice format, not a fully regulation match: there is no net and no let procedure. Standard scoring is used. The camera cannot reliably judge foot faults, service faults, player contact, hindrance or which player won a point, so those decisions require manual confirmation.
